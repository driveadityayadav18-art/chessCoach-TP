# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Dual audience of committed chess competitors:
1. **Club & Intermediate Players (1000–1800 rating)** hitting rating plateaus from rote opening memorization, seeking foundational positional clarity, calculation trees, and pawn structure comprehension.
2. **Title Contenders & Competitive Prodigies (1800–2400+ rating)** requiring high-level tournament preparation, psychological resilience, deep opening ecology, and prophylactic mastery.

## Product Purpose

ChessCoach delivers master-class chess coaching that transforms how chess players calculate, evaluate positions, and build long-term rating growth. Its purpose is to replace brittle memorization with genuine structural mastery, validated through 10 years of international master-level coaching pedagogy.

## Positioning

*"Don't memorize chess. Understand it."*
Unlike generic course libraries, video drills, or robotic engine analysis, ChessCoach uses Grandmaster-grade first-principles pedagogy. It provides the exact methods used to prep international champions and prodigies (including collaborative prep with GMs like Arjun Erigaisi, Praggnanandhaa, and Sam Shankland), tailored into an individualized training system.

## Operating Context

- **Workflow:** Prospective students browse pedagogical pillars and proof, then book a 1-on-1 diagnostic consultation to evaluate their current rating bottlenecks.
- **Environment:** High-end web experience featuring tactile physical/acoustic feedback (haptic-like chess sound toggles), smooth inertial scrolling (Lenis + GSAP), and an interactive 3D Staunton rook (Three.js/OGL).
- **Format:** Bespoke 1-on-1 master coaching, deep game reviews, and customized curriculum roadmaps.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Capabilities:**
  - Dynamic 4-pillar master training framework (Tactical Depth, Endgame Precision, Opening Systems, Positional Mastery).
  - Diagnostic consultation booking flow (`/api/book` with student rating, goals, and obstacle profiling).
  - Interactive tactile web presentation with interactive 3D chess pieces and soundscapes.
- **Constraints:**
  - High editorial standards: dark/light theme harmony, clean typography, avoid tacky gamification or low-effort AI slop.
  - Performance: Smooth 60fps WebGL rendering across desktop and mobile screens.

## Brand Commitments

- **Tone & Voice:** Authoritative, intellectual, pedagogical, calm, and uncompromising on craft.
- **Key Motifs:** Staunton craftsmanship, timeless editorial aesthetics, tactile piece moves, grandmaster rigor.
- **Coach Identity:** Led by Coach Aditya, backed by 10 years of master-level coaching pedagogy and elite tournament preparation experience.

## Evidence on Hand

- Documented collaborative prep pedigree with top international Grandmasters: Arjun Erigaisi, R. Praggnanandhaa, and Sam Shankland.
- Concrete 4-pillar curriculum architecture with candidate move branching and pawn ecology frameworks.
- Production assets: Staunton 3D rook models (`rook.glb`), 2K editorial textures, and bespoke acoustic audio clips.

## Product Principles

1. **Structure Over Memorization:** Emphasize root pawn structures and candidate move logic over rote 25-move engine lines.
2. **Editorial Gravity:** Treat chess as high art and serious discipline—no juvenile gamification badges, arcade sounds, or noisy clutter.
3. **Tactile Interaction:** Give every digital interaction weight, physical momentum, and deliberate feedback.
4. **Individualized Diagnostics:** Every player's rating bottleneck is distinct; coaching begins with structured diagnostic evaluation.
